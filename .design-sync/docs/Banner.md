A flash or callout on the sand band. `info` is the default for status messages and notes; `warn` gets a gold border for something to watch; `danger` is brick for a failure. Copy is one or two plain sentences, no icons, no emoji, no exclamation marks.

```tsx
<Banner tone="warn">Copy this key now. It is shown once and cannot be recovered.</Banner>
```
